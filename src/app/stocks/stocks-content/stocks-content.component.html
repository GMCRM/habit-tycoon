How dividends work

How Stock Dividends Work

Dividends are paid out only when a business owner fully completes their habit goal for the day. The payout depends on:

  • Shares sold: Up to 20 shares per business are available to purchase; each one sold grows the dividend pool investors split
  • Your ownership: The pool is divided proportionally by shares owned, so more shares means a bigger cut
  • Owner's consistency: No completion that day means no dividend — a broken streak also resets the business's stock price

Invest in owners who complete their habits consistently for the steadiest payouts.

How Your Portfolio Works

Your portfolio shows all the stocks you own and tracks your investment performance:

  • Portfolio Value: Total current worth of all your stocks
  • Profit/Loss: How much you've gained or lost since buying
  • Daily Dividends: Money earned when the friends whose businesses you've invested in complete their habits
  • Stock Performance: Track individual stock gains and losses
