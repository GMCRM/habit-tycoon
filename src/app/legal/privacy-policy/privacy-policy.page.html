Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Habit Tycoon ("we", "us", "our") is a gamified habit-tracking app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the app, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information — your email address, display name, and password. Passwords are securely hashed by our authentication provider, Supabase; we never see them in plain text. If you sign up with Google, we receive your name, email, and profile photo from Google instead.
  • Gameplay data — the habits you create, check-ins, streaks, virtual currency, businesses, shop purchases, and achievements within the app.
  • Social data — friend connections, posts, challenges, and reactions you create are visible to the friends you connect with.
  • Purchase data — if you buy virtual currency or premium features, the purchase is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play and managed through RevenueCat. We receive confirmation of the purchase and your entitlement status, never your full payment card details.
  • Feedback — bug reports and feature requests you choose to submit.
  • Device & technical data — basic device and OS information needed to run the app, and network connectivity status.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To operate gameplay — habits, check-ins, the in-game economy, and achievements
  • To power social features you opt into, like friends and challenges
  • To process purchases and unlock what you paid for
  • To respond to feedback and support requests
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the app

4. Third-Party Services

We rely on a small number of service providers to run the app:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and backend functions
  • RevenueCat — subscription and purchase management
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — payment processing
  • Google — if you choose to sign in with Google

Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers above, as necessary to operate the app, or when required by law.

6. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Supabase's managed infrastructure. We use reasonable, industry-standard measures to protect it, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Your Choices & Rights

  • Update your display name and password anytime in Settings
  • Delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete Account. This is permanent.
  • Contact us with any privacy questions or requests

8. Children's Privacy

Habit Tycoon is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data is removed except where we're required to keep it, such as for legal or fraud-prevention reasons.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll update the effective date above and, for material changes, provide notice in the app.

11. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Contact us at support@habittycoon.app.
