🏪 Marketplace

How the Marketplace works

When you or a friend upgrades a business — or deletes a habit — the old business gets listed here for 24 hours. Only you and your accepted friends can see and buy each other's listings.

Streak bonus: a listing's price includes a bonus based on the seller's streak when it was listed, up to +100%.

Buying: tap "Buy" to purchase a listing — the price comes out of your cash. You'll then choose to merge it into one of your existing businesses or start a brand-new habit around it.

Guaranteed payout: businesses listed from a deleted habit are guaranteed — if no one buys them before they expire, the seller is automatically paid out.
